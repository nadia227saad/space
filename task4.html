Explore the Wonders of Space

Space is full of planets, stars, moons, and galaxies that help us understand how the universe works. A space journey teaches us about science, discovery, teamwork, and the future of exploration.


Main Parts of a Space Mission

  1. The Launch Vehicle

    2. the rocket used to carry the payload from Earth's surface into space, overcoming Earth's gravity.

  2. The Spacecraft

    3. The main vehicle that operates in space, which can be a satellite, a robotic probe, or a crewed capsule carrying astronauts.

  3. The Payload

    4. The specific equipment or passengers carried to fulfill the mission's goal, such as scientific cameras, telescopes, sensors, or communication antennas.

  4. Mission Operations / Ground Control

    5. The team of engineers and scientists on Earth who monitor, send commands, and control the spacecraft using deep space tracking networks.

  5. Space Communications System

    6. The radio and antenna system that sends telemetry and scientific data from space back to Earth, and receives commands from ground control.
